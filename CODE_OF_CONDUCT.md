# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We expect all team members to:

- Treat everyone with respect.
- Listen to other team members and allow everyone to contribute.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Communicate problems early.
- Complete their fair share of the work.
- Help other team members when possible.
- Discuss disagreements professionally.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Harassment or discrimination.
- Blaming other team members.
- Ignoring agreed team responsibilities.
- Deliberately disrupting the work of others.

## Resolving Problems

Problems should first be discussed within the team.

If the issue cannot be resolved, it should be raised with the module lecturer or teaching staff.
